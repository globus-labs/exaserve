# ExaServe Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive community behavior includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Showing empathy and taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Considering the well-being of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other behavior that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and maintainer responsibilities

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project
in public. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and taking
appropriate, fair action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may remove
or reject contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when warranted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the current maintainer, Wenyi Wang, at
[wenyiw@uchicago.edu](mailto:wenyiw@uchicago.edu). Do not post sensitive incident
details in public issues or pull requests.

Reports should describe the behavior, its context, and any relevant evidence,
while sharing only the personal information necessary to investigate.
Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy as far as
reasonably possible, and determine a proportionate response. A maintainer
involved in a report should not investigate or adjudicate that report.
Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
